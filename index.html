27.10.2025 — Forever and after.

My Jasmine,

Sometimes I wonder how one person can become such a big part of your world. How someone can turn into your favorite notification, your happiest thoughts, your worries, your dreams, and a part of your future you can no longer imagine without them.

And somehow, that's what you became to me.

Our story hasn't always been easy. We've had beautiful days I wish I could freeze forever, and difficult days when understanding each other wasn't so simple. We've laughed, made memories, disagreed, learned, and experienced things that have shaped our relationship in ways only we truly understand.

But when I think about us, I don't want to remember only the good days or pretend the bad ones never happened. I want to remember our story in its entirety, because every chapter is a part of the journey that brought us here.

I don't expect us to be perfect, habibty. I just hope we never stop learning how to love each other better, understanding each other deeper, and choosing to build something beautiful together.

When I imagine my future, I think about all the little things I still want with you: the memories we haven't made, the dreams we haven't achieved, the ordinary days we'll turn into beautiful ones, and the years from now when we'll look back at where it all began.

If there's one thing I want you to know, it's that you aren't just someone I love today. You're someone I dream of sharing so many tomorrows with.

On 27 October 2025, our story began. And today, I'm grateful for every beautiful memory, every lesson, and every chapter that has been ours.

Happy anniversary, my Jojo. My Jasmine. My Om Luna. 🌙

Whatever pages life has waiting for us, I hope we keep writing them together, with more love, more understanding, and a thousand new reasons to smile at each other.

Me & You. One story. A thousand memories still to come.

Forever and after. ♾️❤️

Yours,

Seif.
